# wa, desu, ja arimasen, ka

## 1. wa (は) - Topic Marker

Structure: [Topic] wa [Info] desu

Example:
- Watashi wa Nihonjin desu

## 2. desu (です) - Is / Am / Are

Example:
- Watashi wa gakusei desu

## 3. ja arimasen (じゃありません) - Is NOT
Negative for

Example:
- Watashi wa sensei ja arimasen. - Main teacher nahi hu

## 4. ka (か) - Question Marker
Sentence ke end me lagao, question ban jayega

## 5. no (の) - Ka / Ki / Ke
Do nouns ko jodne ke liye, malik batane ke liye

Example:
- Watashi no hon desu. - Mera book hai

## 6. mo (も) - Bhi / Also
Wa ki jagah aata hai, jab same cheez dusre ke liye bhi ho

Example:
- Watashi wa gakusei desu. Tomodachi mo gakusei desu.


#7. kore/sore/are

## Affirmative
Kore wa hon desu.

## Interrogative
Kore wa nan desu ka?

## Negative
Kore wa hon ja arimasen.


#8 koko/asoko/doko/soko


## Examples
-- ここは 学校です。(koko wa gakkou desu) - Yahan school hai.
-- そこは 図書館です。(soko wa toshokan desu) - Wahan library hai.
-- あそこは 駅です。(asoko wa eki desu) - Wahan door station hai.
-- どこは トイレですか？(doko wa toire desu ka?) - Toilet kahan hai? - Wrong, sahi neeche hai
-- トイレは どこですか？(toire wa doko desu ka?) - Toilet kahan hai?
