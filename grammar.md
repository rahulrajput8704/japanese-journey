# wa, desu, ja arimasen, ka

## 1. wa (は) - Topic Marker

Structure: [Topic] wa [Info] desu

Example:
- Watashi wa Nihonjin desu

## 2. desu (です) - Is / Am / Are

Example:
- Watashi wa gakusei desu

## 3. ja arimasen (じゃありません) - Is NOT
Negative for

Example:
- Watashi wa sensei ja arimasen. - Main teacher nahi hu

## 4. ka (か) - Question Marker
Sentence ke end me lagao, question ban jayega
