# wa, desu, ja arimasen, ka

## 1. wa (は) - Topic Marker

Structure: [Topic] wa [Info] desu

Example:
- Watashi wa Nihonjin desu

## 2. desu (です) - Is / Am / Are

Example:
- Watashi wa gakusei desu

## 3. ja arimasen (じゃありません) - Is NOT
Negative for

Example:
- Watashi wa sensei ja arimasen. - Main teacher nahi hu

## 4. ka (か) - Question Marker
Sentence ke end me lagao, question ban jayega

## 5. no (の) - Ka / Ki / Ke
Do nouns ko jodne ke liye, malik batane ke liye

Example:
- Watashi no hon desu. - Mera book hai

## 6. mo (も) - Bhi / Also
Wa ki jagah aata hai, jab same cheez dusre ke liye bhi ho

Example:
- Watashi wa gakusei desu. Tomodachi mo gakusei desu.


#7. kore/sore/are

## Affirmative
Kore wa hon desu.

## Interrogative
Kore wa nan desu ka?

## Negative
Kore wa hon ja arimasen.
